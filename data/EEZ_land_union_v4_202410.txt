the following attribution:

  Exclusive Economic Zones (version 4). Available online at

Country attributes (ISO_A2, CONTINENT, REGION_UN, SUBREGION), the canonical
country names, and the Antarctica polygon come from Natural Earth
ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson, which is in the public domain.
